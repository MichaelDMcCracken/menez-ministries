Scope of Ministry

Where we serve

From rural America to the remotest part of the earth.

Domestic Ministry (“Flat Rabbit Ministries”)

Scott and Gina Menez serve as area missionaries in Southwestern Idaho and Southeastern Oregon in the high desert country. In Acts 1:8, we find our Lord commanding His disciples to take the unashamed gospel “to Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, and even to the remotest part of the earth.” That command has been passed down from disciple to disciple throughout the church age.

We often think of “the remotest part of the earth” as the jungles of the Philippines—we have been there too—but there are still many remote areas within the United States that have very little gospel witness and very few Christ-exalting, Word-saturated established churches or Bible studies. Scott and Gina seek to bring the good news of the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ to these remote areas.

Throughout the last 40 years of Scott and Gina’s ministry, they have been prepared to accurately handle God's Word while exalting a Christ-centered biblical theology in order to evangelize the lost and encourage and teach the saved to “Love Christ, Love His Word, and Love His people.”

The mission field

A circuit-riding ministry in the high desert.

Southwestern Idaho and Southeastern Oregon are extremely rural where farming and ranching are the main way of life. There are few incorporated towns in these areas and often just small gatherings of ranches that require an extensive amount of travel to reach them.

Scott is the pastor of a few small churches that struggle to maintain enough people to support a pastor. And yet, there is a great need for teaching, discipleship, and shepherding in these little high desert places of worship. This means Scott and Gina have a circuit-riding ministry that moves throughout their mission field as they encourage and shepherd people in the remotest part of the earth.

God has not only given Scott and Gina a great desire for these people who live in remote places, but He has also allowed them to learn their way of life and understand their daily pressures of raising America's food supply in a growing hostile climate against the farming and cattle industry. This often opens opportunities to share the truth of God's Word with those throughout this vast ministry field.

Around the world

Training pastors and encouraging their wives.

Throughout the years of ministry, Scott and Gina have travelled internationally to many places training pastors and their wives. This ministry has brought great joy to our hearts, and we give God all the glory for allowing us to pour truth, instruction, and shepherding into the lives of these precious men and women around the world.

The financial gifts you give to this ministry will allow us to continue to travel: for Scott to train national pastors and for Gina to encourage their wives. Their international ministry consists of several one- or two-week trips a year to ministry partners around the globe. However, the bulk of their ministry remains on the mission field in Southwestern Idaho and Southeastern Oregon.

We count it great joy to see the gospel of Jesus Christ being preached, the lost drawn to Christ, the saved growing in the grace and knowledge of the Lord Jesus Christ, as He builds His church from every tribe and tongue and people and nation—including rural America.
