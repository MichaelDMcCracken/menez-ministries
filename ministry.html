Who we are

A ministry for places that are hard to reach.

Flat Rabbit Ministries is the ministry of Scott and Gina Menez, serving rural communities in Idaho and Oregon. We desire to make Christ known and to faithfully teach the Word of God wherever He sends us.

Through preaching, discipleship, encouragement, and practical care, we partner with local churches and believers to see the gospel go forward.

Scope of ministry

Faithful ministry, wherever God opens the door.

01

Proclaiming the gospel

Pointing people to the hope of Jesus Christ through the clear teaching of Scripture.

02

Strengthening the church

Serving local churches through preaching, teaching, and encouragement.

03

Reaching the remote

Taking the good news to rural communities and the remotest part of the Earth.
